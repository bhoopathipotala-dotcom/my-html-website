System science

What makes the solar system so fascinating

Gravity, atmosphere, seasons, and moons all shape each world differently.

🪐

Gravity shapes everything

The pull of gravity governs orbits, tides, moon motion, and even how planets form and evolve over time.

🌤️

Atmospheres create climate

Some planets are shielded by thick skies while others have little protection, changing how heat and weather behave.

🌙

Moons add drama

Many worlds have moons that influence motion, tides, and the stories of space exploration itself.
