Legendary missions

Mission Timeline

From lunar landings to the farthest telescope ever launched.

6.5+ decades of nonstop exploration
12 human missions beyond Earth orbit
3 major rover missions now active
1969

Apollo 11

First humans landed on the Moon, making history and inspiring the world with a giant leap. The mission used lunar modules, precise navigation, and a carefully timed landing sequence to carry astronauts safely to the surface.

1977

Voyager 1

Sent back stunning views of the outer planets before entering interstellar space with a golden record. Its radio communications and long-term power systems allowed it to function far beyond its original design goals.

2012

Curiosity Rover

Explored Gale Crater on Mars and uncovered evidence that water once flowed there. The rover used onboard chemistry tools and cameras to study rocks and soil in remarkable detail.

2021

James Webb Space Telescope

Changed astronomy by peering into the earliest chapters of cosmic history. Its infrared eyes allow scientists to see through dust clouds and observe distant galaxies with extraordinary clarity.

2021

Perseverance Rover

Collects samples on Mars while preparing the way for future human exploration. It uses advanced autonomy, scientific instruments, and a helicopter companion to learn more about ancient environments.

2024

Europa Clipper

Set to explore an icy moon that may contain the ingredients for life beneath its frozen crust. Its instruments will scan the surface and subsurface to detect signs of oceanic activity and chemistry.

Mission science

Why each mission matters

Every launch expands our understanding of the universe in a new way.

🌙

Lunar discovery

Human and robotic missions to the Moon have revealed geology, history, and the long-term potential of off-world living. They help us study the Moon’s surface, understand how planetary bodies form, and test systems needed for future long-term stays.

🔭

Deep space observation

Modern telescopes and probes uncover the oldest stars, distant galaxies, and clues about how the cosmos began. These missions work by capturing different wavelengths of light and sending data back across immense distances.

🚀

Future exploration

Today’s missions prepare for longer journeys, better habitats, and life-support systems beyond Earth. They combine robotics, AI, materials science, and careful planning so future explorers can travel farther and stay longer.

Major achievements

How these missions changed science

Some missions made history, while others opened new doors for the future.

🧪

Discovering signs of ancient water

Rover missions on Mars have shown that water once existed there in larger quantities. This helps scientists understand climate change on other worlds and how life might have emerged in the past.

📡

Reaching beyond the solar system

Voyager missions carried humanity’s message into deep space. Their long-distance communication systems and durable design proved that exploration can outlive its original timeline.

🌌

Seeing farther into the universe

Space telescopes like Webb are helping scientists observe the early universe, study planetary atmospheres, and search for conditions that could support life elsewhere.
